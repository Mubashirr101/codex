> _"Knowledge is not merely collected—it is fortified."_

Welcome to **Citadel**, my digital fortress of knowledge.

This vault is where I document everything I learn, build, break, and rebuild. Instead of letting useful information disappear into bookmarks, browser history, or forgotten notes, I preserve it here in a structured, searchable, and interconnected knowledge base.

Whether it's a machine learning algorithm, a Linux command I always forget, a development setup, or notes from a research paper,if it's worth remembering, it belongs in the Citadel.

---

## ⚔️ Purpose

The goal of this vault is simple:

- Build a second brain for technology.
    
- Learn in public through well-organized notes.
    
- Connect concepts instead of storing isolated information.
    
- Create documentation that my future self will actually enjoy reading.

A fortress is built stone by stone.

Likewise, expertise is built note by note.

This vault is not intended to be perfect. It is intended to grow. Every article is a snapshot of what I know today and may evolve as I learn more tomorrow.

If a note helped you solve a problem or understand a concept, then the Citadel has served its purpose.
